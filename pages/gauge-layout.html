Automatic Gauge Takeover

Critical engine information can temporarily override the manual layout.

How it behaves

Manual: pick COOLANT before a hard pull and coolant becomes the large focus gauge.

Automatic: if coolant reaches the configured high limit, COOLANT takes over until temperature drops back below the threshold.

Priority: oil pressure, coolant temperature, then battery voltage.
